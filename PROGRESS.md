## How to update this

1. Open the NeetCode 250 dashboard, read the gauge and the per-topic rows.
2. Overwrite the numbers here. Don't append, don't keep history — this file is a snapshot.
3. Re-sum the Solved column against the overall figure. If they disagree, trust the dashboard.
4. Update the **Last synced** date.
5. Log the problem itself in `solved.md`, and the one-line state in `RESUME.md`.

Record a broken streak as a plain `0`. It's information, not a verdict.
