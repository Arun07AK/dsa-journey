The whole algorithm, in room language

2 = a person who knows. Orange, fidgety, ready to talk.
1 = a person who hasn't heard. Blue, sitting quietly. This is what the counter tracks.
0 = an empty chair. Nobody to tell. Whispers die there.
The queue = the line. Everyone who knows stands in it, in the order they learned.
One whistle = one BFS level. The line is frozen at the whistle: only the people already standing there talk this minute.
Nobody is told twice. The moment you hear it you turn orange, so a second whisper just bounces off you.

Why the whistle is frozen: if new people could talk the instant they heard it, minute 1 and minute 2 would smear together and the minute count would mean nothing. Freezing the line at the whistle — for _ in range(len(q)) — is the clock.

Two ways this ends. Everyone has heard it → stop whistling immediately, even if people are still standing in line, because they have nobody left to tell. Or the line empties while someone is still blue → they are walled off by empty chairs and can never hear it → -1.
